The Firm

About Ambray Law & Advisory

Legal representation and advisory work for individuals, companies and institutions.

Ambray Law & Advisory is a Philippine law firm based in Alabang, Muntinlupa City.

The firm's work includes corporate and commercial matters, taxation, litigation and dispute resolution, and regulatory advisory.

Led by Atty. Aquino F. Ambray, CPA, the firm is positioned to consider both the legal and accounting aspects of matters where these disciplines intersect.

Legal matters are handled by the firm's counsel, with administrative support for client coordination, documentation and case management.

Our Practice

Practice Areas

Our work covers the following areas, subject to the nature of each engagement and the firm's assessment.

01

Corporate & Commercial Law

Company formation, corporate governance, contracts, shareholder arrangements and commercial transactions.

02

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Advice and representation in civil and commercial disputes, applicable court proceedings and negotiated settlements.

03

Taxation & Tax Advisory

Tax assessments and disputes, BIR proceedings, tax refund and credit claims, VAT-related matters and tax considerations in commercial transactions.

04

Regulatory & Compliance Advisory

Corporate compliance, regulatory obligations, and legal matters involving relevant Philippine government agencies.

Services are described in general terms. The firm determines whether it can accept a particular engagement after an initial review.
