Our Standard

The Ambray Diamond Standard

The gold diamond in the Ambray mark is not a decoration. It is a declaration. Four principles face the client from every direction, governing how this firm conducts every engagement. Each principle is active, not aspirational. You will find evidence of all four in every brief we write, every opinion we render, and every hearing we attend.

North Point · I
Accuracy
Precision and Thorough Analysis
We read the contract. We read it again. We read the regulation it references, and then the regulation that regulation refers to. Accuracy is a professional obligation. Advice built on incomplete analysis transfers risk from counsel to client, and that is not counsel at all.
East Point · II
Acumen
Judgment and Practical Strategy
The letter of the law does not always answer the question the client is actually asking. We combine legal knowledge with commercial judgment to close that gap. A technically correct answer that cannot be executed is an incomplete answer. Our advice is designed to work in the world as it is.
West Point · III
Advocacy
Conviction and Integrity
We stand for our clients in negotiations, in hearings, and in every communication that carries their interests. Advocacy is not aggression. It is the disciplined, strategic use of legal authority directed with full conviction toward a client's legitimate objectives. We do not hedge when we should be holding.
South Point · IV
Accountability
Ethics and Ownership
Every matter we accept is a matter we are accountable for, completely. We do not pass responsibility or allow deadlines to become negotiable. When something requires our attention, it receives it. The firm's reputation is built on the consistency with which we meet our obligations to every client.
At the center of the diamond is the client and the matter entrusted to the firm. Every principle, from every direction, is aimed at that center. This is what we mean by the Ambray Standard.
Practice Areas

Four areas of practice. One standard of counsel.

The firm's practice covers the legal needs of businesses and individuals at consequential moments. Each area is handled in-house by lawyers who hold both legal qualification and CPA certification, without referral to outside counsel.

01
Corporate and Commercial Law
We advise on the full lifecycle of a business, from how it is formed to how it is governed, grown, restructured, or sold. Our corporate counsel is grounded in both legal structure and commercial reality.
  • Company formation and structuring
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and due diligence
  • Shareholders' agreements and governance
  • Commercial contracts and joint ventures
  • Cross-border transactions and foreign equity compliance
02
Litigation and Dispute Resolution
Every hearing we attend is built on thorough preparation. We appear at all levels of the Philippine court system and in arbitration and mediation proceedings, with an approach that is strategic and relentless on the client's behalf.
  • Civil and commercial litigation
  • Criminal defense and prosecution support
  • Arbitration and domestic mediation
  • Injunctions, provisional remedies, and enforcement
  • Appeals before the Court of Appeals and Supreme Court
03
Taxation and Tax Advisory
Tax advice from lawyers who are also CPAs carries a different weight. We resolve exposure before it becomes liability, and we plan with the full scope of the Philippine tax framework in view, not just the provision immediately in front of us.
  • BIR assessments, protests, and settlements
  • Tax planning and transfer pricing advisory
  • Estate and succession planning
  • VAT compliance and refund claims
  • Tax implications of corporate transactions
04
Regulatory and Compliance Advisory
Regulated industries require counsel that understands both the requirement and the consequence of non-compliance. We advise on SEC and BSP regulations, data privacy obligations, AML compliance, and the governance structures that give institutions confidence in their own operations.
  • SEC and BSP filings and compliance
  • Data Privacy Act compliance and DPO advisory
  • Anti-money laundering program design
  • Corporate governance frameworks
  • Industry-specific regulatory compliance
