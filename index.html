The Firm

Who We Are

Ambray Law & Advisory is a full-service legal practice based at the 17th Floor of the Insular Life Corporate Centre in Alabang, Muntinlupa.

The firm offers full-service legal counsel across five areas of practice: corporate and commercial law, litigation and dispute resolution, taxation and tax advisory, regulatory and compliance advisory, and notarial services. We serve individuals, businesses, and institutions who require sound, reliable legal guidance handled with care and confidentiality.

Every matter accepted by the firm is handled with the same standard of attention, regardless of scale or complexity.

Atty. Aquino F. Ambray holds both legal qualification and CPA certification, giving the firm a practical edge in matters where law and finance are closely linked. The firm is supported by a professional team in legal operations, business development, client services, and regulatory coordination.

What We Do

Every matter is approached with the full attention of the firm's counsel, bringing expertise and care to each aspect of a client's case from start to finish.

01
Corporate and Commercial Law
Company formation, mergers and acquisitions, shareholders' agreements, commercial contracts, and cross-border transactions.
02
Litigation and Dispute Resolution
Civil and commercial litigation, criminal defense, arbitration, mediation, and appeals before all levels of the Philippine courts.
03
Taxation and Tax Advisory
BIR assessments, tax planning, transfer pricing, estate and succession planning, VAT compliance, and tax implications of corporate transactions.
04
Regulatory and Compliance Advisory
Corporate governance, data privacy, and sector-specific compliance before the SEC, BSP, and other government agencies, under Philippine and applicable international regulations.
05
Notarial Services
Notarization of legal instruments, contracts, affidavits, deeds, and other documents requiring authentication.
