The Firm

Who We Are

Ambray Law & Advisory is a boutique legal practice headquartered at the 17th Floor of the Insular Life Corporate Centre in Alabang, Muntinlupa.

The firm offers full-service legal counsel across four areas of practice — corporate and commercial law, litigation and dispute resolution, taxation and tax advisory, and regulatory and compliance advisory. We serve individuals, businesses, and institutions who require sound, reliable legal guidance handled with care and confidentiality.

Every matter accepted by the firm is handled with the same standard of attention, regardless of scale or complexity.

Atty. Aquino F. Ambray holds both legal qualification and CPA certification, giving the firm a practical edge in matters where law and finance are closely linked. The firm is supported by a professional team in legal operations, business development, client services, and regulatory coordination.
