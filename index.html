Our Standard

The Ambray Diamond Standard

The gold diamond in the Ambray mark is a declaration. Four principles govern every engagement — Accuracy, Acumen, Advocacy, and Accountability — each directed toward the client at the center. This is not a values statement. It is how we practice law.

Accuracy
Precise, thorough, and grounded in the facts. We read everything, and we read it carefully. Advice built on incomplete analysis is not advice.
Acumen
Legal knowledge combined with commercial judgment. A technically correct answer that cannot be executed is an incomplete answer.
Advocacy
We stand for our clients with conviction and strategy. We do not hedge our positions when we should be holding them.
Accountability
Every matter we accept is one we are fully accountable for. We own our work, we meet our commitments, and we deliver.
Four principles. One standard. Every matter entrusted to Ambray Law & Advisory is measured against all four — without exception.

Corporate and Commercial Law
We advise on the full lifecycle of a business, from how it is formed to how it is governed, grown, restructured, or sold.
  • Company formation and structuring
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and due diligence
  • Shareholders' agreements and governance
  • Commercial contracts and joint ventures

02
Litigation and Dispute Resolution
We appear at all levels of the Philippine court system and in arbitration and mediation proceedings, with an approach that is strategic and relentless on the client's behalf.
  • Civil and commercial litigation
  • Criminal defense and prosecution support
  • Arbitration and domestic mediation
  • Injunctions, provisional remedies, and enforcement
  • Appeals before the Court of Appeals and Supreme Court
03
Taxation and Tax Advisory
Tax advice from lawyers who are also CPAs. We resolve exposure before it becomes liability, and we plan with the full scope of the Philippine tax framework in view.
  • BIR assessments, protests, and settlements
  • Tax planning and transfer pricing advisory
  • Estate and succession planning
  • VAT compliance and refund claims
  • Tax implications of corporate transactions

Regulatory and Compliance Advisory
We advise on SEC and BSP regulations, data privacy obligations, AML compliance, and the governance structures that give institutions confidence in their own operations.
  • SEC and BSP filings and compliance
  • Data Privacy Act compliance and DPO advisory
  • Anti-money laundering program design
  • Corporate governance frameworks
  • Industry-specific regulatory compliance
